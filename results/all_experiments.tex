\noindent\textbf{Dataset:} XLE (Yahoo Finance, 2020-01-01 onward), 80/20 chronological split.
\textbf{Metric:} Annualised Sharpe $= \sqrt{252}\cdot\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{sign}(\hat{s})\cdot r_t]/$std$(\cdot)$.
WF~Sharpe is mean walk-forward Sharpe over expanding folds (``---'' = not computed).
Status: \textbf{best} = best committed model; keep = improved over prior; --- = discarded.
Sessions: Det-S1 = alternative-data experiments (Apr~20); S1--S3 = autoresearch loop;
Sweep = HP grid; Post = post-cleanup; Ablation = structured Phase~2 ablations.
